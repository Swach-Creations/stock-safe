StockSafe

Stock-out and expiry alerts for small pharmacies, delivered on WhatsApp and SMS.

A medicine can exist in the supply chain and still fail the patient at the shelf. StockSafe makes stock levels and expiry dates visible before they become failures — with a routine that takes seconds.

  • 01
    CaptureEvery stock-in and sale logged in seconds, by scan, tap, or a 30-second count.
  • 02
    StoreOne shared batch-level record per item. Entries queue offline and sync later.
  • 03
    AnalyseDays of stock left, expiry risk, and a right-sized reorder, worked out daily.
  • 04
    ActOne ranked digest each morning, on the channel pharmacists already use.
